Frequently Asked Questions & Formulas

Q: What is the formula for Percentage Increase or Decrease?

Percentage Change = ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100%. A positive result indicates a percentage increase, while a negative number denotes a percentage decrease.

Q: How is sales tax calculated after a discount?

In most jurisdictions, sales tax is applied to the discounted sale price: Final Price = (Original Price - Discount Amount) × (1 + Tax Rate). Our calculator shows exact savings automatically.
