📝 Character Count vs Word Count

Platforms like X (formerly Twitter), SMS gateways, and Google Meta Descriptions enforce strict character limits (including or excluding whitespace), while academic essays and publishers benchmark by total word count. Our dual tracking guarantees compliance across both standards.

⏱️ Words Per Minute (WPM) Benchmarks

The average adult reads silently at approximately 200–250 words per minute. For spoken delivery (podcasts, YouTube voiceovers, TED talks), the standard speaking rate is typically 130–150 WPM. Use our reading estimate to calibrate your pacing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How are sentences and paragraphs identified?

Sentences are recognized by terminal punctuation marks (periods, exclamation marks, question marks) followed by whitespace or ending boundaries. Paragraphs are recognized by non-empty blocks separated by line breaks.

Q: What are the ideal word count targets for SEO articles?

Comprehensive informational articles that rank on Google page 1 typically average between 1,200 and 2,200 words. Shorter answers or news pieces perform best around 600–800 words, provided the search intent is thoroughly resolved.

Q: Does this tool work offline?

Yes! Once loaded, the script runs entirely on your local machine with zero external API calls. You can disconnect from WiFi and continue analyzing your documents without disruption.
