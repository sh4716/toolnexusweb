Mortgage Formula (Fixed Rate Amortization)

Monthly payment is calculated using the standard formula: M = P [ i(1 + i)^n ] / [ (1 + i)^n - 1 ], where P is principal, i is monthly interest rate, and n is total months. All calculations occur locally in your browser.
