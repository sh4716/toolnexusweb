Salary Calculations & Working Hours FAQ

Q: What is the 2,080 working hours rule?

A full-time standard schedule of 40 hours per week across 52 weeks equals exactly 2,080 hours per year. This is the official baseline used by HR, payroll, and the US Department of Labor.

Q: What is the difference between Bi-Weekly and Semi-Monthly pay?

Bi-weekly pay occurs every two weeks (26 paychecks per year, with two months containing three paychecks). Semi-monthly pay occurs twice per month (24 paychecks per year, usually on the 15th and last day).
