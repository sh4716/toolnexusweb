Tipping Etiquette & Common Questions

Q: What is standard tipping etiquette in the US and Canada?

Casual dining or takeout is typically 10%-15%; standard sit-down dinner service is 15%-18%; excellent or fine dining is 20%-25%.

Q: How does rounding up work?

Enabling "Round up" raises each person's share to the next whole dollar (e.g. $24.35 becomes $25.00), preventing awkward loose change and adding a small bonus tip for the server.
