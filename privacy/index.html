Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026 · Effective Immediately

🛡️ Our Core Privacy Commitment: 100% Client-Side Architecture

Unlike traditional online utility websites, ToolNexus never uploads your files, images, documents, passwords, or calculation inputs to any backend server. All operations (including PDF rendering, image compression, cryptographic hashing, and financial calculations) are computed locally inside your device's browser memory (via WebAssembly and standard Web APIs).

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because all our tools run exclusively in your browser, we do NOT collect, store, or process:

  • Contents of your uploaded PDF documents, images, certificates, or text files.
  • Inputs entered into financial calculators (such as mortgage amounts, salary details, tip values).
  • Passwords generated or cryptographic tokens (JWTs, hashes) analyzed.
  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) without your explicit consent.

2. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

To maintain our services completely free without charging subscriptions or paywalls, ToolNexus works with third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

Google and DoubleClick Cookies:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of the DoubleClick DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Local Storage

We use standard HTML5 localStorage purely to remember your preferences (such as your chosen Light/Dark theme and recent tool settings). This data remains strictly in your browser and is never transmitted to any external server.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you have full rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Know & Access: Since we do not maintain accounts or personal databases, we hold no user profiles.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell your personal data. You can control advertising cookies via our cookie preference settings or your browser privacy controls.

5. Contacting Us

If you have any questions, compliance requests, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy officer at:
Email: privacy@toolnexusweb.com (or via our Contact Form).
